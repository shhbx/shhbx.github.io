Multichannel Speech Enhancement with Spatial-Aware Explicit Phase Estimation

Hong-Hao Sun, Yang Ai, Hui-Peng Du, Zhen-Hua Ling, and Wu Guo
Paper Code

Abstract

Existing neural-network-based multichannel speech enhancement methods often rely on magnitude or real-imaginary modeling, but pay insufficient attention to explicit phase reconstruction and the effective exploitation of spatial information and geometric diversity of microphone arrays, which limits overall enhancement performance. To address these issues, we propose a novel Multichannel Speech Enhancement model with Spatial-Aware Explicit Phase Estimation (SAPE-MSE). The model adopts a magnitude-phase encoder-decoder architecture, where multichannel degraded magnitude and phase spectra are encoded and processed using improved time-frequency Transformers. Parallel decoders estimate clean magnitude and phase spectra at the reference channel, followed by inverse transformation to reconstruct clean speech. Importantly, the phase decoding is spatial-aware, guided by inter-channel phase difference features and circular array spatial processing. Experimental results demonstrate that SAPE-MSE outperforms representative multichannel speech enhancement methods, achieving superior PESQ performance on an eight-channel synthetic dataset.
